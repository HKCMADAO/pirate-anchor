# Pirate Anchor

Portrait one-finger swing game. Hold anywhere to throw the anchor at the nearest anchor point, let go to fly off along the swing. Twenty levels across five chapters. Reach the treasure chest to clear a level; collect all three doubloons and beat par for three stars.

Play: https://hkcmadao.github.io/pirate-anchor/

Best on a phone in portrait. On desktop, hold the mouse button to hook. Progress is saved in your browser.
